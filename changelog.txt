*** Changelog ***

* Fix : Compatibility for Odoo 19.0 series
* Fix : Plugin has been made compatible with the shop's default PayPal payment
* Enhanced : iDEAL payment logo and title to iDEAL | Wero
* Enhanced : Improved checkout payment form validation and optimized JavaScript to enhance iframe loading performance
* Removed : Sofort, Giropay, Barzahlen/viacash and Payconiq payments
